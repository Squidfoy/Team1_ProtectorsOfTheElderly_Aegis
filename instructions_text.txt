To get the most accurate fall detection, please follow these Camera Placement Guidelines:

1. Mount the camera at a 45-degree angle:
        From a wall or corner, roughly 6-8 feet off the ground. This gives the best view of the full body.
2. Do not mount the camera on the ceiling. 
        A top-down view makes everyone appear horizontal and will cause constant false positives.
3. Do not mount the camera directly facing where the person will walk toward it. 
        Falls toward the camera are harder to detect than side-on falls.
4. Ensure the camera has a clear, unobstructed view of the monitored area. 
        Objects blocking the frame will reduce detection accuracy.
5. Avoid placing the camera behind glass:
        Reflections can distorte the camera's view and affect detection quality.
6. Ensure adequate lighting. 
        The system works best in well-lit environments. Avoid placing the camera where it will be looking directly into a bright light source.
