Aegis is designed for practical home use but has several known limitations that users and caretakers must be aware of:

1. The accuracy is lower for falls toward the camera direction and falls where the person's back is fully facing away from the camera. 
2. Ceiling-mounted camera views from the top-down perspective are not supported in Aegis.
3. The falls may not be detected if the person's body is partially blocked by furniture or other objects.
4. Slow movements to the floor may occasionally trigger a false positive (Someone lying down or kneeling down to look under furniture).
5. Aegis is optimized for monitoring one person at a time. Accuracy may decrease in scenes with multiple people.
6. Dark environments, flickering lights, may reduce detection accuracy.
7. Unusual camera angles may affect accuracy. Standard wall or corner-mounted cameras are greatly recommended. 

This application is not intended to replace professional monitoring, it's recommended to use other fall detection devices with it.
